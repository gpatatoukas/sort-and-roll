# Sort & Roll Google Ads images

This set contains 20 visual concepts in three Google App campaign image sizes, plus a curated folder of 20 upload candidates.

## Folders

- `landscape/` — 20 JPEGs, 1200 × 628 (1.91:1)
- `square/` — 20 JPEGs, 1200 × 1200 (1:1)
- `portrait/` — 20 JPEGs, 1200 × 1500 (4:5)
- `selected-20/` — 7 square, 7 landscape, and 6 portrait files, one per distinct concept
- `source/masters/` — original square concept artwork

All JPEG exports are below Google Ads’ 5 MB per-image limit. The wide and portrait layouts preserve the full square artwork in the center, using a softened crop of the same artwork to fill the extra space.

## Copy ideas

Suggested short headlines (Google App campaign headline limit: 30 characters):

- Free Color Sort Puzzle
- Sort Colors, Unwind
- A Fresh Puzzle Every Day
- 99-Step Weekly Challenge
- Sort & Roll: Bottle Puzzle

Suggested descriptions (90 characters or fewer):

- Sort colorful bottles in quick, satisfying puzzles whenever you have a moment.
- Discover a fresh color-sort challenge every day.
- Take on the 99-step weekly journey, one satisfying pour at a time.
- Plan each move, reveal hidden colors, and sort every bottle.
- Play Sort & Roll free. Easy to start, satisfying to master.

## About the performance reference

The supplied Block & Roll report showed strong results for “Free Block Puzzle Game” and descriptions about quick satisfying play and daily/weekly challenges. Its image rows were identified only by orientation, not by creative, so this set follows the broader messaging themes without attributing performance to any particular image concept.
