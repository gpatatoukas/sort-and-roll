# Sort and Roll image generation prompts

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Artwork was then resized and
composited with `scripts/generate-branding.cjs`. These are the original prompts
used to create the source masters in `assets/branding/source/`.

## Bottle emblem

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single polished mobile puzzle game brand mark for Sort and Roll, a colourful relaxing water-sort game. Transparent background, no text, no lettering, no enclosing app tile. Centered compact emblem: three chunky clear glass potion bottles with smooth rounded shoulders, two upright bottles clustered close, one small bottle tilted above them pouring a single smooth curving turquoise stream into the center bottle. Attractive coral, mint turquoise and lilac layered liquids, warm golden highlights. Premium playful softly dimensional 3D mobile game illustration, clean readable silhouette, thick glass rims, restrained glossy reflections, soft internal shading, front three-quarter view. The emblem should read clearly at tiny app icon sizes, avoid excessive bubbles, sparkles, detached small decorations and thin details. Fill central 75% of a square canvas, keep complete bottle silhouettes within frame. No words, no watermark, no logos from existing games.

## App icon

Use case: compositing. Create the final square mobile app icon for Sort and Roll using the provided transparent bottle emblem as the edit target. Preserve the identity of the three bottles, liquid colours and pouring stream exactly. Place the whole mark centered with comfortable 12% clear margins on ALL sides, no cropped glass or stream. Add a rich dark midnight indigo full-bleed square background with a restrained soft violet halo behind the bottles and subtle turquoise glow at the base, creating strong small-size readability. Opaque background across every pixel, no transparency, no rounded outer corners, no outer frame, no lettering, no watermark. Premium polished playful puzzle game icon.

## Google Play feature graphic

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a landscape Google Play feature graphic, aspect ratio 1024:500 (approximately 2.048:1), for the mobile water-sort puzzle game Sort and Roll. Use the supplied bottle logo as a supporting reference and preserve its identity, layered coral mint lilac liquids, rounded glass and pouring turquoise stream. Place that complete emblem prominently in the right half, occupying approximately 75% of the graphic height and wholly inside the canvas. On the left half render the exact title 'Sort and Roll' as two confident lines, 'Sort' above 'and Roll', in chunky beautifully rounded off-white display lettering. Below in small clear type render exact tagline 'A little pour. A lighter mind.' Elegant midnight indigo background, soft lavender atmospheric halo, a few restrained light dots near the bottles. Keep all text and bottles inside a generous 7% safe margin. Premium playful calming aesthetic, clear highly readable at small sizes. No app icon tile, no phone mockup, no fake gameplay, no buttons, no ratings, no awards, no store badges, no extra wording or watermark.
